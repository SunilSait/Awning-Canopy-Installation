Our Story

From a Small Workshop to California's Leading Shade Experts

ShadeCraft was born in 2009 from a simple belief: every outdoor space deserves exceptional shade solutions that combine beauty, durability, and innovation. Our founder, Marcus Blackwell, started with a two-person team and a passion for transforming patios, decks, and commercial spaces.

We've completed over 4,800 installations, earned an industry-leading 4.9-star rating, and built partnerships with the world's finest material suppliers, including Sunbrella®, Dickson®, and Somfy® motorized systems.

Our Journey

Milestones That Define Us

A look back at the key moments that shaped ShadeCraft into the company it is today.

2009

The Beginning

Marcus Blackwell founds ShadeCraft in a small Greenfield workshop with a two-person crew and a vision for premium outdoor shade solutions.

2012

500th Installation

We complete our 500th project and earn our first "Best of Houzz" Service Award, solidifying our reputation across California.

2016

New Facility

We move into our 18,000 sq ft state-of-the-art fabrication facility with CNC machinery, automated cutting, and a dedicated design studio.

2019

Smart Home Integration

ShadeCraft launches its SmartShade™ line — IoT-enabled motorized awnings compatible with Alexa, Google Home, and Apple HomeKit.

2022

Sustainability Pledge

We become the first awning company in the US to achieve ISO 14001 certification and announce our commitment to carbon-neutral operations by 2028.

2024

West Coast Expansion

ShadeCraft expands service coverage across the entire West Coast with new teams in Oregon, Washington, and Nevada — now serving 4,800+ happy clients.

Meet Our Founder

The visionary behind ShadeCraft's commitment to excellence and innovation.

Marcus Blackwell

Founder & CEO

With a background in structural engineering and a lifelong passion for outdoor design, Marcus brings over 20 years of industry experience to ShadeCraft. He's a certified installer with IFAI (Industrial Fabrics Association International), holds a B.S. in Civil Engineering from UC Berkeley, and has personally overseen more than 1,200 installations.

Under his leadership, ShadeCraft has grown from a two-person operation to a team of 65+ specialists, earning recognition from Houzz, the BBB, and Angi with consistent 5-star ratings across all platforms.
